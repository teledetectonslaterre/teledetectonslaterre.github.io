In July 2025, Google released a new AI model and associated dataset called <a href="https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaearth-foundations-helps-map-our-planet-in-unprecedented-detail/">Alpha Earth Foundations</a> (AEF). This model produces a dataset that can be used in remote sensing for machine learning. At the time, I was working on the detection of abandoned vineyards with optical and radar data, so I decide to test my method on this new data source. And I quickly got pretty good results!<br><br>
